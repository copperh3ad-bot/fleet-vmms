Your Fleet Maintenance Manager

Know what you're spending on your fleet's maintenance, and why.

Fleet OTG replaces spreadsheets, phone calls and chat approvals with one job card for every repair, so you can see what was done, what it cost and who said yes.

1

Every approval on record

Job cards, quotations and invoices are approved with the person's name and the time. Only the administrator may decide on their own request, and it is recorded the same way.

2

One card per repair

Status, parts, labour and cost on one card. Every step is logged: who did what, and when.

3

Workshops quote from a phone

The workshop gets a one-time link on its own phone number, sends its estimate, and it is recorded against the job card number.

4

Cost lands on the right vehicle

Spend is counted per vehicle and per period against its budget, and follows who held the vehicle at the time.

5

Repeat repairs are flagged

The same fault coming back on the same vehicle is shown, so you can see what keeps failing and why.

6

Drivers see their own vehicle

A driver sees the job cards of the vehicle they hold and what it has cost them, and nothing else of the fleet.

7

Fill it in offline

Download the standard job card form as Excel, fill it in at the workshop with no internet, and upload it later.

8

Every person has an employee code

Each login is tied to an employee code and a role in your organisation, so each action traces back to a real person.
