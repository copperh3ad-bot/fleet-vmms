What it costs to run

One company with 200 vehicles: the monthly operating cost

Fleet OTG is multi-tenant: every company has its own workspace on one shared platform. So most cost is a small fixed platform bill that every extra company shares, and only a thin slice grows with each company. These are planning estimates from stated assumptions, in US dollars a month.

  • ~200 job cardsa month
  • ~400 documentsworkshop estimates and invoices read by the AI

  • Pro planAI on; all job cards by WhatsApp (worst case)

What moves the number

  • WhatsApp is the lever. Meta’s Pakistan rates are $0.015 for utility messages and for replies after the first 1,000 a month; marketing is $0.0473. Fewer status messages, or leaving the monthly odometer request off, cuts the largest line.
  • The platform steps up, it does not scale per company. Expect a larger database tier somewhere past 25 to 50 busy companies.
  • Each extra 200 vehicles in one company adds about $10 to $18 a month on Basic and $10 to $40 on Pro; the shared platform bill does not grow. Pro is one price for every fleet size, so the margin narrows on very large fleets. AI stays small.
  • Not counted: staff time, support, taxes, and any paid plan for live chat.

Where it is used use cases

Delivery and logistics fleetsVans and trucks on the road all day. Job cards with approvals, repeat-repair flags, odometer by phone link.Fewer repairs paid twice.
Manufacturers and exportersPool cars and staff transport. HR vehicle requests, optional trip requests, cost by department.One record of who had which vehicle.
Warehouses, yards and constructionLoaders and forklifts serviced by engine hours as well as kilometres and months.Service on hours, not guesswork.
Distribution and field salesMany drivers, few office staff. Readings and papers tracked, reminders before anything expires.Papers and licences never lapse unseen.
Companies with many workshopsWorkshops send estimates from their own phones. Quotations compared with invoices before payment.No workshop has to learn a system.
Multi-department organisationsEach department looks after its own vehicles within a limit, while finance sees the whole fleet.Approvals match who is accountable.
