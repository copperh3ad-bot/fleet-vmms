**Deviations and assumptions, stated plainly**

2. `scripts/set-plan.mts --plan pro --tenant qa-ff-fleet` only writes `tenant_subscriptions`; the Pro gating reads `public.org_billing.plan_key`. I set `org_billing.plan_key='pro'` and platform setting `trial_max_vehicles=60` by SQL on the throwaway database (the e2e suite does the same for the plan). See F-16.
3. `dept_approval_limit` (50,000) and `module_trips` (1) have no admin screen, so I wrote them into the workspace `settings` table by SQL, as `e2e/s13` does. See F-03. The maintenance-policy rules (replace after 7 years, 3 free accidents, 18 months between, driver share 30%) were entered through the Policy screen from `tests/fixtures/policy-text.pdf`, which is a fictional "Acme" policy and is the only written policy available (the owner's own `Vehicle Policy._0001.pdf` in the repo root is a scanned image and was not read).
4. KPI history (Phase 6) was seeded **before** the approvals, so the approver could see the scorecard when choosing a workshop. The seed (`p6seed.mts`) refuses to run unless the target is the `QA-FF Fleet` workspace on `fleet_vmms_e2e`, uses the repo's `pgDriver` and `withTenant`, and disables the guard triggers of five history tables inside its own transaction (events cannot be back-dated through the app); 39 closed historical cards on 39 inactive `HIST-*` vehicles. Resulting scorecard (from the system's own `workshop_scorecard`): Alpha 12 jobs, 3.0 days, quote-vs-bill 0%, rework 0%; Bravo 10, 5.0, +4%, 10%; Charlie 8, 2.5, +12%, 13%; Delta 9, 9.0, +28%, 33%.
5. Four extra pool vehicles `LPL-4121..4124` (no holder, Logistics department) were added because 20 vehicles all held by requesters leave the pool empty. Total vehicles in workspace: 24 real plus the 39 seeded.
6. Quote and invoice amounts in Phase 5 were computed from the original card estimate, so card 6's quote (28,000) ended up above the edited estimate (19,000); this is a data artefact I then used (see F-09).
7. The "return an invoice to the workshop" step was not executed correctly by my script and is reported as SKIPPED. Link expiry was tested by moving `expires_at` into the past in the throwaway DB.

**Data produced:** 24 vehicles (20 requested: 10 by form, 10 by Excel import with a bad file rejected first), 20 job cards then 3 repeat cards, 5 HR requests, 5 trips, 2 cargo requests, 21 workshop invoices, 1 provider trip invoice.

## 3. GAP list

1. **Purchase booking from an HR request.** Exists: status `purchase_approved` (button "Approve the purchase", note only), then "Give a vehicle from the pool" once a vehicle is added by hand; history events with person and time. Missing: supplier, quotes, estimated cost, approval against a budget, purchase order, expected delivery date, link to the vehicle record that will be created, notification to the requester at each step, and a way to cancel the booking. Suggested scope: a "Purchase" panel on a request with supplier quotes (reuse the single-use quote link from ADR-078 for dealers), a cost and budget line, approval by Fleet manager plus finance for amounts above a limit, "vehicle received" which creates the vehicle through the normal add-vehicle form and gives it to the employee in one step.
2. **Vacant-vehicle-at-loading-location cargo decision.** Exists: cargo request form, manager accepts or rejects, "Make a trip from this", the trip page then offers assignment from all vehicles or third-party quotes; a pool flag on vehicles; `base_location` on vehicles. Nothing looks at the pick-up place or at which vehicles are free: the cargo page shows no vehicle suggestion at all, and the decider has to know which vehicle is vacant. Suggested scope: on a cargo request, list active vehicles whose base location matches the pick-up place (fuzzy, then manual choice), not on a trip or in the workshop in the pick-up window, right class/capacity; if none, offer "Ask logistics providers" pre-filled from the cargo fields. Needs a capacity field on vehicles and a normalised location list first.
3. **More than one workshop quoting the same card** and quotes beside KPIs (F-06).
4. **Payment recording and payment reports** for workshop and provider invoices (F-05).
5. **Seats, capacity and papers on pool vehicles** in the HR and trip pickers (F-07, F-08, F-14).
6. **Company-side activity log for the auditor** (ADR-070 is operator-only).
7. **Applying written policy rules on cards and invoices** (F-11), including the written policy being silent on repeats (Q-03).
8. **WhatsApp notice for cargo:** nothing was written to the WhatsApp file outbox for cargo requests (0 messages); the WhatsApp templates are env-driven, so this may be a configuration gap in my environment; not proven either way.
9. **Reopen a rejected card** (F-15).

## 4. Policy questions for the owner

- Q-01: May the Tenant Admin approve other people's job cards, approve quotations and check invoices when a Fleet manager exists? The permission table says yes; ADR-075/077 read as no. (F-01)
- Q-02: Should Logistics decide HR vehicle requests and assign vehicle plus driver on trips, as described? Today only Tenant Admin and Fleet manager can. (F-02)
- Q-03: The written policy is silent on repeat work. Who pays when a repeat repair is needed on the same fault within 60 days: the earlier workshop (warranty), the new workshop, the driver, or the company? Expected answer to my reading of the policy and ADR-009: the system flags repeats and asks about the workshop's guarantee, the policy only splits non-consumable repairs 70% company / 30% employee (clause 4.3) and says nothing about rework, so the company has no written basis to refuse payment of the repeat invoice and the system does nothing to stop it. Please decide the rule (suggest: a repeat on the same fault inside the window by the same workshop is not payable until the workshop confirms the guarantee has lapsed, a repeat by a different workshop is payable but charged back to the earlier workshop's next invoice).
- Q-04: Does "poor previous repair" count as a repeat even when the original reason differs? Today the flag needs the same reason code.
- Q-05: Is there a tolerance for invoices above quote (workshop and provider), and who is the second approver above it?
- Q-06: What happens to quotes far above the card estimate or above the vehicle budget: block, or require a second approver?
- Q-07: Should vehicles with expired papers, or in a workshop job, be unassignable for trips and HR requests?
- Q-08: Is the Logistics approval limit 50,000 meant to cover the quotation as well as the estimate (ADR text says both)? I observed the manager approving all quotes; the Logistics quote path above the limit was not exercised.

## Executive summary

1. Environment: local throwaway e2e stack only; nothing near production; mail and WhatsApp to files.
2. 392 steps: 363 ok, 14 FAIL (about 10 distinct defects), 11 GAP, 4 SKIPPED (AI features and the Resend inbound reply need keys that do not exist locally; one step my script botched).
3. No tenant leak, no forbidden write by finance, auditor, HR or requester, and no payable without a checked invoice were found. Segregation of duties, link single-use, over-limit refusal and double-booking refusal all work.
4. The administrator can approve other people's job cards (F-01), contrary to the intent of ADR-075/077; owner decision needed.
5. A Pro company cannot switch Trips on or set the Logistics limit from any screen (F-03).
6. Logistics cannot decide HR vehicle requests or assign vehicle and driver on trips as the owner describes (F-02).
7. Repeat repairs are flagged only for the same reason code, and finance's invoice screen shows no repeat or warranty notice, so a repeat invoice is payable unflagged (F-04); the written policy is silent.
8. There is no payment recording at all, and each card gets only one workshop quote, so quotation judgement against KPIs is not possible (F-05, F-06).
9. Pool vehicles with expired insurance or an open repair can be assigned to trips and employees without warning (F-07, F-08).
10. Both owner-described features (purchase booking from an HR request, vacant-vehicle-at-location cargo decision) do not exist beyond a status and a manual trip; see the GAP list.
