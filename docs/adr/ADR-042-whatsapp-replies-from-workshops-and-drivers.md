# ADR-042: Workshops and drivers answer on WhatsApp

Status: accepted, 2026-10-29. Builds on ADR-033 (workshop email replies), ADR-041 (WhatsApp sending) and ADR-006 (the driver odometer link). Owner request: "Fleet OTG will also need replies from vendors and drivers using WhatsApp for relevant tasks."

## Decision
1. **Fleet OTG asks on WhatsApp itself, and the answer comes back to Fleet OTG.** Staff press "Ask on WhatsApp" on a job card. The server sends an approved **utility template** to the workshop's number (name, company, "estimate" or "invoice", job card number) with a button to the one-time link (ADR-030, ADR-033). The workshop may use the button, or simply **reply to the message** with a PDF or photo, or with a short text such as "ready" or "need 2 more days". The link is never shown to the person pressing the button.
2. **Which request a reply answers is the message it replies to.** WhatsApp's "reply" carries the id of our message. Sending the request stores a hash of that id (prefixed `wa:`) where an emailed request keeps its reply code (`quotation_links.reply_code_hash`) and a token route of kind `reply`, so a reply is found with the code that email replies already use. A message that is not a reply to one of ours is **not filed anywhere** (the same number can work for several companies, so guessing would be wrong); the sender gets one hint an hour: "please use Reply on that message".
3. **A reply is filed exactly like an emailed one** (`src/lib/vendorflow/whatsapp-inbound.ts`, the same three database functions run as the `inbound_mail` role): the message and its photos and PDFs on the job card, flagged "needs entering", the people who decide are notified, nothing is read out of the message, a repeated delivery files once, at most 5 replies per request, nothing is filed once the request is closed. Staff enter the figures, or the AI reader (ADR-034) drafts them for a person to approve. A short text update is filed the same way and shows on the job card; it changes no figure and approves nothing. The sender is thanked: "We have your message for job card JC-1042."
4. **The address Meta calls** is `/api/inbound/whatsapp` (CLAUDE.md rule 5): open to the internet but useful to nobody except Meta. Every call must carry `X-Hub-Signature-256`, an HMAC of the exact body with the app secret (`WHATSAPP_APP_SECRET`), compared in constant time; without it, 401. `GET` answers Meta's one-time check only for `WHATSAPP_VERIFY_TOKEN`. Messages not sent to our own number, receipts, voice notes and anything malformed are ignored, never an error. Pictures and files are fetched with our token, only from Meta's own addresses, up to 4 MB, and only photos and PDFs are kept (the file's own bytes are checked, not its name).
5. **Settings** (Netlify, never the repository): `WHATSAPP_APP_SECRET`, `WHATSAPP_VERIFY_TOKEN`, `WHATSAPP_REQUEST_TEMPLATE` (the approved request template, with one URL button). The button is offered only when WhatsApp is set up and the template is named. No new migration for workshops.

## Not done (next)
- **Drivers' odometer readings** are not built yet. The plan: the odometer request goes as a template with the single-use link (ADR-006); a driver's reply with a reading (text or a photo of the dashboard) becomes a pending odometer submission for that request, to be confirmed by staff. It needs a second template and its own database step like the workshop one.
- Replying to drivers' or workshops' free chat with anything other than the acknowledgement; delivery and read receipts (ignored).
- Voice notes and locations are not filed.

## Cost
Meta charges per business-started conversation (the template). Replies inside 24 hours, including the acknowledgement, are free of a new conversation charge.
