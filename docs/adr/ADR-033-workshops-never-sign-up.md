# ADR-033: Workshops are added by the organization and never sign up

Status: accepted, 2026-10-28. Extends ADR-030 (the estimate link). Retires the workshop sign-up and the workshop login portal.

## Decision
1. **An organization adds its own workshops** on the Vendors page (name, mobile number, email). A workshop never creates an account and never signs
   in. The public "Join as a workshop" pages and the workshop portal (`/w/<slug>/vendor/...`) are retired.
2. **Everything a workshop sends comes through one of two routes, and both end up on the job card:**
   - **A one-time link** (phone or email). `public.quotation_links` gains `kind` (`quotation` or `invoice`). The estimate link is ADR-030. The new
     **invoice link** opens `/q/<token>` with the lines of the *approved* estimate pre-filled, so the workshop bills against them; extra lines are
     allowed and show to the person who checks as not in the estimate. A workshop cannot press "start" or "complete", so sending its invoice records the
     work as started and done (the job card history says so) and moves the card to "invoiced", where the signed-in route would end up.
   - **A reply to an autogenerated email** (phase 2, below).
3. **Same safety rules as ADR-030, kind by kind.** Only the SHA-256 of the token is stored; the control plane (`link_routes`, kind `quotation` or
   `invoice`) finds the organization; the route runs as the `quotation_link` role, which reads no table and can only execute SECURITY DEFINER functions
   with a pinned search path (`invoice_link_info`, `submit_invoice_via_link`; the estimate function now also requires `kind = 'quotation'`). Unknown,
   used, expired and revoked links look the same. A refused submission does not burn the link, and a workshop that made a fixable mistake is told what
   to fix rather than that the link is dead. An estimate link cannot send an invoice and the reverse.
4. **Sending the link.** By phone: the sender's own WhatsApp or messages app opens with the text ready (the link is never shown). By email: the server
   sends it to the address on the Vendors page, so the link reaches only the address the organization entered.
5. **Replying by email (phase 2).** Each email carries a unique reply address on a subdomain (`reply.fleetotg.com`), received by Resend. A signed webhook
   hands the message to the app, which files the message and its attachments on the job card as *received by email, needs entering*, and tells the
   people who handle it. **Staff enter the figures**; nothing is read out of a PDF automatically, because a misread amount on an invoice is worse than a
   minute of typing. Decided by the owner on 2026-10-28: file it, staff enter the figures; straight onto the job card, flagged for review.

## Why
Workshops are small businesses that will not keep another login. A link on a phone, or an email they can answer, is what they will actually use,
and it keeps every estimate and invoice recorded against a job card number with no account to manage or to leak.

## Consequences
- Rule 5 (CLAUDE.md) lists the invoice link with the estimate link; phase 2 adds the signed inbound-email webhook.
- Existing workshop logins and the vendor portal are removed in phase 3 (a migration is not needed: the data stays; only the pages and the join flow go).
- The Privacy policy lists Resend as the provider of inbound email once phase 2 is live.
