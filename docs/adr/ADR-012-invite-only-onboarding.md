# ADR-012: Invite-only onboarding and plan limits

Status: accepted, Phase 9b (owner decision, 2026-10-22: invite only, no self-service sign-up).

- **Workspaces are created by the operator, from a script** (`scripts/create-workspace.mts`), not from a web page. The script makes an empty workspace, optionally puts it on a plan, and prints one invitation link for its first administrator. There is no sign-up page and no screen that can create a workspace, so there is nothing for a stranger to find.
- **Nobody joins without an invitation.** Administrators (`admin.users`) invite people from the Users screen: name, email, role, which vehicles they can see; for the workshop role, which workshop. The link is random (32 bytes), single use, valid 7 days, stored only as a hash, shown once. One open invitation per email.
- **Accepting needs two things: the link or the invitation, and a confirmed email that matches.** A person with a link creates their account on `/invite/<token>` with the invited email (it cannot be changed), or, if they already have an account, signs in and accepts on the first page, which lists invitations addressed to their confirmed email. Holding the link alone is not enough to join under another email.
- **Exception to rule 5 (CLAUDE.md updated):** `/invite/<token>` is the second open page, after `/o/<token>`. It shows the workspace name, the invitee's name and email, and one password form. Its guard is an exact pattern (a 43-character token), it is rate limited (shared across servers), says the same thing for unknown, used, expired and revoked links, is not cached or indexed, and the token is never logged. Creating the login uses the sign-up call of the identity provider with the invited email only; an account with no invitation has no access to anything.
- **Accepting is one transaction:** membership, employee record (an existing record with the same email and no login is linked rather than duplicated; workshops get no employee record but are linked to their vendor record), and the invitation marked used. If anything fails, nothing is created and the invitation stays open.
- **Safety rules for access changes:** you cannot change your own access, workshop roles can only be made by invitation, and the last active administrator cannot be removed. Disabling someone takes effect on their next request.
- **Plan limits are enforced in the database,** so no screen, import or script can go past them: `max_vehicles` by a trigger on vehicles (sold vehicles do not count; CSV import is all or nothing), `max_users` by a trigger on memberships (workshop logins do not count). The invitation screen also counts open invitations so a seat is not promised twice. No plan, or a plan without that limit, means no limit. Limits are numbers on a plan; there are no prices anywhere (`scripts/set-plan.mts` sets them).
- **Demo data** (`scripts/seed-demo.mts`, `src/lib/demo/seed.ts`) goes through the same services as the screens, creates people without logins, and refuses any workspace that already has vehicles.
- **Not built:** a platform administrator screen, billing, changing a workspace's plan from the web, email delivery of invitations (the link is copied or sent from the administrator's own email or WhatsApp), phone-only invitations, removing a person's login from the identity provider when they are disabled.
- **Depends on the identity provider's settings:** the sign-up call must be allowed, and if "confirm email" is on, new people confirm by email first and then accept from the first page. Not verified against the real project yet (it needs a test email address).
